Framework v1.3.0

Released: 31 July 2026  ·  Scope: Ongoing / hygiene.

Source-citation and reporting improvements across the whole framework:

  • Source URLs on every regulation. All 10 regulations now carry a sources[] array pointing at EUR-Lex, legislation.gov.uk or the standing supervisor (FCA, PRA, BoE, EBA, ENISA, European Commission, EDPB, ICO). No blog posts, no vendor whitepapers, no LLM summaries.
  • Framework version + review date now stamped on every report cover, so any customer question can be traced back to a specific build.
  • RAG accuracy fix. The regulation RAG rating used to return "green" if a regulation was referenced in the question set — even if the customer hadn't answered any of those questions. Now correctly returns "grey — not answered" until at least one question is answered.
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

Framework v1.2.0 — Phase 3

Released: 31 July 2026  ·  Scope: UK AI advisory regime.

Added UK AI as a new regulation — the tool now surfaces UK AI expectations for any UK-established firm using AI/ML:

  • ICO Guidance on AI and data protection — DPIA and individual-rights expectations
  • FCA supervisory expectations for AI in regulated activities — consumer duty, senior-manager accountability
  • PRA / Bank of England SS1/23 — model risk management for AI / ML in banks and insurers
  • New Q_G4 question captures principles alignment — Aligned / Partial / None / N/A
  • Auto-selected on any UK footprint; added to finserv + insurance sectors
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

Framework v1.1.0 — Phase 2

Released: 31 July 2026  ·  Scope: Screening completeness.

Additive expansion — the tool now covers many more real-world scenarios without changing any existing finding:

  • Q_C1: seven new cloud providers — Oracle Cloud (EU / global split), IBM Cloud (EU / global), Salesforce / Heroku, Snowflake, Databricks. All with CLOUD Act findings.
  • Q_C2 matrix extended — new rows for AI/ML training data and government / classified data; new "UK region only" column between EU-only and eu-backup.
  • Q_A2 DORA entity list — Payment Institutions / EMIs and CASPs (crypto) split out from the "other-fin" catch-all with their own findings (PSD2 + DORA; MiCA + DORA).
  • Q_G1 wording fix — Annex III items 1, 4, 5(b), 5(c) explicitly cited (biometric identification, employment/HR, credit scoring, life/health insurance pricing).
  • Q_G3 GPAI question — captures use as downstream / provider / integrator, triggering AI Act Chapter V (Arts. 51–56) obligations differently by role. Article 25 provider-substitution risk called out.
  • Q_H2 EUCS Basic assurance level — added the third EUCS tier alongside Substantial and High.
  • Q_J1 DORA incident RTS — now cites Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301 with the 4h / 72h / 1-month cadence.
  • Q_B1 financial-data anchor — refined from a stretched DORA Art. 5 reference to Art. 3 (definitions) + Art. 5, plus banking secrecy and PCI DSS where relevant.
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

Framework v1.0.0 — Phase 1

Released: 31 July 2026  ·  Scope: Legal defensibility.

Four items that fixed customer-visible defective outputs from the initial DR&J review:

  • EU-US Data Privacy Framework now handled explicitly. New "SCCs via DPF" option in Q_D1; new "US cloud with DPF-certified processor" column in the Q_C2 matrix; DPF variants for Q_F1 (backups) and Q_G2 (AI compute). DPF-certified US transfers now scored as low severity (adequacy) not critical.
  • Q_C1 GCP split — "Google Cloud — EU regions only" (positive) vs. "Google Cloud — includes non-EU regions" (high). Fixes systemic false-positive for every GCP-EU customer.
  • Three new regulations — EU Data Act (Reg. (EU) 2023/2854), UK Operational Resilience (FCA PS21/3; PRA SS2/21), UK Critical Third Parties (FSMA 2023 Part 9). Each with a new question (Q_D3, Q_F5, Q_I3) and appropriate auto-selection rules.
  • Q_H3 sovereign-controls follow-up — multi-select capturing which controls (EU-only personnel, HYOK keys, anti-disclosure clauses, EU sub-processor list, local operating entity) are actually in place. "None of the above" surfaces a high-severity finding — closes the Q_H1 "US with sovereign boundary" false-pass.
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

About this changelog

Every entry cites primary sources. EUR-Lex for EU legislation, legislation.gov.uk for UK legislation, or the relevant standing supervisor (FCA, PRA, BoE, EBA, EIOPA, ENISA, European Commission, EDPB, ICO). No blog posts, no vendor whitepapers, no LLM-generated summaries as authorities.

Refresh cadence. Every regulation change is tracked and reviewed at least quarterly by a named owner. Adequacy decisions, phased commencement dates, CJEU rulings, new supervisory guidance — all logged.

Nothing here is legal advice. The disclaimer on every report page applies to everything on this page.
