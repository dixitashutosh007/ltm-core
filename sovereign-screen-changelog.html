Framework v1.3.0

Released: 30 July 2026  ·  Scope: Additive — no existing regime call changed.

Five new EU regime blocks added, each with primary-source citations and a matching readiness question:

  • EUCS — EU Cybersecurity Certification for Cloud Services (Regulation (EU) 2019/881; ENISA scheme). Informational for EU financial firms sourcing non-EU cloud, and for cloud providers considering certification.
  • PSD2 / PSD3+PSR — Payment Services (Directive (EU) 2015/2366; PSD3/PSR in EU legislative process). Applies to payment institutions, EMIs and credit institutions with EU nexus.
  • CRR / CRD + EBA cloud outsourcing — Banks (Reg. 575/2013; Dir. 2013/36/EU; EBA/GL/2019/02; EBA/GL/2019/04). Applies to EU credit institutions.
  • Solvency II — Insurers (Dir. 2009/138/EC; Del. Reg. 2015/35 Art. 274; EIOPA cloud outsourcing GL BoS-20-002). Applies to EU insurers and intermediaries.
  • eIDAS 2 — EU Digital Identity Wallet (Reg. (EU) 2024/1183). Informational for financial firms and their ICT providers, preparing for Wallet-acceptance obligations by end-2026.

Inventory: 16 regimes · 47 questions · 22 facts · all 16 regimes carry authoritative source URLs.

Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

Framework v1.2.0

Released: 30 July 2026  ·  Scope: Additive — screening completeness.

Three new questions and seven new rules wiring four previously-unused facts. No existing regime call changed.

  • Biometric KYC question — fills a critical AI Act Annex III item 1 gap for FS firms using biometric identification.
  • Employment AI question — Annex III item 4 (recruitment, work allocation, performance evaluation).
  • GPAI role question — feeds three new AI Act Chapter V rules (downstream user, provider, integrator).
  • DORA Art. 16 microenterprise regime now surfaces for firms < 10 staff and ≤ €2m turnover / balance sheet (per Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC).
  • GDPR Art. 9 special-category rule (EU + UK mirror) — adds DPIA (Art. 35), DPO (Art. 37(1)(c)), Schedule 1 DPA 2018 references.
  • UK Op Res proportionality rule for micro / small UK financial firms.
  • GDPR one-stop-shop rule for firms operating in two or more EU Member States (Art. 56; EDPB Guidelines 8/2022).
  • Large-scale processing rule for firms holding > 1 million records (Art. 30, Art. 35, Art. 37(1)(b); WP 248 rev.01).
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

Framework v1.1.0

Released: 30 July 2026  ·  Scope: Legal defensibility.

Eight items that fixed customer-visible defects from the v2 review:

  • EU-US Data Privacy Framework now handled explicitly. New follow-up question when data is hosted in the US; DPF-certified recipients no longer flagged as needing legal review.
  • Sovereign-offering false-pass fixed. "Yes — sovereign offering" no longer scores 100 without evidence; a new maturity rating captures the actual controls.
  • US cloud coverage widened. Oracle Cloud, IBM Cloud, Salesforce/Heroku, Snowflake, Databricks now correctly trigger CLOUD Act detection.
  • UK AI advisory regime added — ICO / FCA / PRA SS1/23 principles-based expectations.
  • MiCA regime added for crypto-asset service providers (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114).
  • Grade labels renamed — "Needs legal review" → "Confirm with counsel"; "Advisory" → "Informational".
  • "Unknown" on CTP dependency now surfaces as "Confirm with counsel" rather than silently dropping out.
  • UK CTP readiness question now shown to ICT providers as well as financial entities.

About this changelog

Every entry cites primary sources. EUR-Lex for EU legislation, legislation.gov.uk for UK legislation, or the relevant standing supervisor. No blog posts, no vendor whitepapers, no LLM-generated summaries as authorities.

Refresh cadence. Every regulation change is tracked and reviewed at least quarterly by a named owner. Adequacy decisions, phased commencement dates, CJEU rulings, new supervisory guidance — all logged.

Nothing here is legal advice. The disclaimer on every report page applies to everything on this page.
