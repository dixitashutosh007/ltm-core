Framework v1.0.1 — Phase 1

Released: 31 July 2026  ·  Scope: Legal defensibility.

Four items that close labelling and mis-quoting risks identified in the initial deep review:

  • BFSI sector gate. New Sector classification dropdown ({Banking / Financial Services · Other sector}). The BFSI structural uplift (+13 percentage-points at zero capability, scaled down with maturity, capped at 45%) now applies only when a customer classifies as BFSI. Non-BFSI customers get the base waste band with no sector adjustment, and the report basis line reflects that.
  • Currency freeze on report render. The report's currency is now snapshotted the moment the value case is generated, and shown on the cover ("Currency £ GBP"). Previously, changing the reporting currency after generating the report silently re-denominated every figure — a £2m estimate could become $2m by clicking a dropdown.
  • Not-financial-advice language added to the disclaimer: "Indicative and directional only. Not a commitment, quote or financial advice." Closes the reading that a CFO could treat a range on an LTM-branded PDF as a savings quote.
  • Framework version + reviewed date on the cover — Framework v1.0.1 · reviewed 2026-07-22 · Currency £ GBP. Every value case is now traceable back to a specific build.
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

Framework v1.0.0 — Initial release

Released: 22 July 2026  ·  Scope: Baseline.

First customer-facing release of the FinOps Value Case — a pre-maturity qualification instrument that sizes the unrealised value at stake from cloud waste, so the decision to commission a formal FinOps engagement is grounded in a number, not a hunch.

  • 29 questions across estate, visibility, predictability, ownership, controls and value.
  • 5 weighted scoring signals — visibility, predictability, control, ownership, value realisation.
  • Waste bands anchored to Flexera 2026 State of the Cloud (n=753), the Flexera multi-year 27–32% band (2019–2026), the Harness 2025 21% floor and the FinOps Foundation State of FinOps 2026.
  • "Don't know" is a first-class answer — DKs render a distinct "Your blind spots" section on the report and downgrade the confidence label from "Reasonable" to "Moderate" or "Low".
  • 3-year exposure compounds annual growth to show the cost of a three-year delay in acting.
  • Recommended service areas triggered by the customer's own weakest signals, re-ordered by their stated concerns.
Legal review sign-off: Ashutosh Dixit (ashutosh.dixit@ltm.com)

About this changelog

Every entry cites primary sources. Flexera State of the Cloud reports, FinOps Foundation State of FinOps, Harness Cloud Cost Management report — the industry-standard public benchmarks. LTM's own directional adjustments (the BFSI uplift factors) are labelled as such in the report basis line, not passed off as published sector waste rates.

Refresh cadence. Reviewed at least quarterly by a named owner. Any new Flexera State of the Cloud release or FinOps Foundation update triggers an out-of-cycle review.

Nothing here is financial advice. The disclaimer on every report page applies to everything on this page.
