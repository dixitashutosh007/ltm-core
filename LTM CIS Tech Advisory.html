The Brief

Enterprise IT and infrastructure decisions have become board-level questions — regulatory, economic and architectural all at once. CIS Tech Advisory helps enterprises answer them fast: where the estate stands today, what to do about it, and how to keep control of data, systems and spend as the environment changes.

Public Cloud

Turn cloud from a technology footprint into a governed source of business value. From the anchor-cloud decision to workload placement, FinOps, AI-ready foundations, and architectural health — advisory that decides what runs where, spends intelligently on every dollar and every token, and modernises with a plan the board can fund.

Right Cloud Strategy & Workload Placement

  • Determines the right primary (anchor) cloud and the deliberate balance across multiple clouds
  • Places each workload where it belongs — public, private, hybrid or repatriated — weighed across value, resilience, sovereignty, latency and data gravity
  • Surfaces lock-in and exit risk early, with first-pass mitigation for the largest hyperscaler dependencies
  • Defines a target cloud operating model and guardrails to prevent accidental multicloud sprawl
  • Produces a defensible placement heatmap and anchor-cloud stance the board, procurement and regulators can back

FinOps Excellence

  • Establishes a maturity baseline across five stages — from Chaos to Automated — with capability-level scoring
  • Analyses cloud bill and usage data to surface prioritised savings opportunities with dollar-impact bands
  • Designs the cultural side of FinOps — roles, cadence, showback and chargeback — alongside the technical optimisation
  • Extends governance beyond cloud to AI, GenAI, SaaS and licensing spend, aligned to FOCUS data standards
  • Delivers a Crawl-Walk-Run elevation roadmap and KPI scorecard the finance and engineering teams jointly own

AI-Ready Cloud Foundations

  • Reviews the cloud and data landscape to determine whether AI at scale is genuinely supportable today
  • Surfaces gaps in landing zone, data platform, MLOps posture and inference footprint that block AI adoption
  • Identifies foundational investments needed before AI use cases can safely reach production
  • Aligns cost, responsible-AI and EU AI Act guardrails into the cloud foundation from day one
  • Produces a readiness scorecard and prioritised remediation plan against the intended AI use-case portfolio

Cloud Strategy & Adoption Roadmap

  • Establishes a coherent cloud strategy aligned to business goals — not a set of disconnected point moves
  • Assesses readiness across strategy, applications, infrastructure, security, operating model, FinOps and skills
  • Produces a directional 6R / 7R disposition per application at a portfolio level
  • Sequences Immediate, Short-term and Long-term actions with owners, dependencies and cost bands
  • Delivers a board-ready adoption narrative and a mobilisable first phase ready to fund

AI-Powered Well-Architected Review

  • Assesses infrastructure and priority workloads against hyperscaler well-architected principles across six pillars
  • Uses AI-assisted analysis to review far more configuration and telemetry than a manual review of the same duration
  • Prioritises findings across reliability, security, cost, operations, performance and sustainability
  • Surfaces cost and security quick wins early — a natural tie-in to FinOps and resilience workstreams
  • Establishes a repeatable baseline to track architecture health over time as the estate evolves
Digital Sovereignty

Sovereignty is now a board-level operating question, not a compliance footnote. With DORA, NIS2 and the EU AI Act converging through 2026, advisory that answers it clearly — where the organisation stands, what to do about it, and how to keep data, systems and operations under control if regulatory or geopolitical conditions change.

Quick Sovereignty Posture Assessment

  • Delivers a fast, board-ready read on sovereignty exposure — before any budget is committed
  • Cuts through the confusion between "data residency" and true digital sovereignty
  • Assesses posture across seven lenses: strategic, jurisdictional, data, cloud, data centre, AI, and operational resilience
  • Creates a single common view across teams hearing DORA, EU AI Act, NIS2 and EUCS separately
  • Produces a 30 / 60 / 90-day no-regret action list and a first-pass hyperscaler concentration scorecard

Sovereignty Readiness Assessment

  • Moves the organisation from awareness to a defensible, evidence-based readiness baseline
  • Produces a detailed sovereignty risk register scored against regulatory and operational impact
  • Co-creates target-state principles and guardrails with IT, security, legal and business stakeholders
  • Delivers a costed, sequenced 12–18 month remediation roadmap tied to 2026 regulatory deadlines
  • Provides a first-pass exit or multi-vendor strategy to reduce hyperscaler concentration risk

Application Portfolio Mapping & Roadmap

  • Maps the application portfolio against sovereignty, business-criticality and modernization lenses
  • Assigns a clear per-application disposition — the "6 Rs" mapped explicitly to sovereignty need
  • Sequences a wave-based modernization and migration roadmap that de-risks execution
  • Identifies sovereignty-sensitive and high-criticality applications needing action first
  • Attaches indicative effort, complexity and cost bands — a ready input to the business case

Regulatory Alignment & Exit Strategy

  • Maps the current estate against DORA, NIS2, the EU AI Act and EUCS in a single alignment view
  • Assesses hyperscaler and third-party ICT concentration risk at the workload and contract level
  • Develops first-pass exit and multi-vendor options aligned to sovereign cloud and open-source alternatives
  • Supports the case for sovereign cloud partnerships without vendor lock-in
  • Produces evidence and narrative that stands up to regulator, auditor and board scrutiny
AI Infusion

Turn scattered AI experiments into a prioritised, funded and governed adoption path. Advisory that identifies where GenAI and agentic AI will move the numbers, assesses whether the data and cloud foundation can support them, and builds the responsible-AI guardrails needed for a 2026 regulatory environment — from EU AI Act obligations to internal accountability.

AI Opportunity & Value Advisory

  • Reviews process and value-chain landscape to surface high-value AI infusion opportunities
  • Scores use cases across value, effort, risk and data-readiness — not tech for its own sake
  • Distinguishes GenAI, agentic AI and classical ML use cases and their respective adoption paths
  • Prioritises a first wave of use cases with expected outcomes and KPIs the business will recognise
  • Provides directional build-buy-partner options and platform choices across hyperscaler and specialist AI

AI Readiness & Data Foundation Assessment

  • Assesses whether the existing data and cloud foundation can support AI at meaningful scale
  • Surfaces gaps in data quality, classification, lineage and platform capability that block adoption
  • Reviews AI-relevant cloud posture — model hosting, inference footprint, data movement and cost
  • Identifies foundational investments needed before AI use cases can safely reach production
  • Produces a readiness scorecard and prioritised remediation list against the intended use-case portfolio

Responsible AI & Governance Advisory

  • Maps AI use cases against EU AI Act risk categories and high-risk system obligations from August 2026
  • Establishes a responsible-AI governance model — roles, review cadence, escalation and audit trail
  • Defines guardrails across bias, transparency, human oversight and data handling per use-case class
  • Aligns responsible-AI governance with existing risk, security and compliance functions
  • Delivers an AI governance blueprint the CIO, CISO, legal and business can jointly operate

AI Cost & FinOps for AI

  • Extends FinOps discipline to AI, GenAI and agentic workloads before spend becomes unmanageable
  • Establishes tagging, allocation and forecasting practices for token, inference and training cost
  • Surfaces early optimisation opportunities across model choice, caching, prompt design and platform
  • Aligns AI spend to business value with per-use-case unit economics and ROI tracking
  • Prepares the organisation for AI cost governance to become a board-level metric
